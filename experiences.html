Research

My research interests lie on the interdisciplinary multiphysical hydrodynamic transport at micro-/nanoscale, covering physico-chemical and electromechanical hydrodynamics. Typical systems include electrokinetics at electrolyte solution interface and electron hydrodynamics in 2D pure conductors. The perspectives of kinetic theory and multiscale modeling are taken. Simulation and/or experiment are conducted when necessary.

PCH
Physico-Chemical Hydrodynamics at micro-/nano-scale, e.g. electrokinetic transport
QH
Quantum Hydrodynamics in solids, e.g. electrons and phonons
AM
Atomic Migration in multiphase solid media, e.g. electro- and thermomigration
CF
Complex Flows in porous media, e.g. particles and emulsions
MS
Modeling and Simulation of multiphysical hydrodynamic transport, kinetic and multiscale
